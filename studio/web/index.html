Describe a system's behavior - a rate limiter, a payment flow, a replication protocol - and the assistant will interview you, draft a SpeckDL spec, compile it, and verify it with a real Z3 solver. Then it will push back: missing invariants, unintended nondeterminism, edge cases you haven't covered.
